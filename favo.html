My Favorite Things ♡

Everyone has little things that make their days better, and these are some of mine! From music I love listening to, apps I use every day, to movies I enjoy watching. Here are three things that make me happy!


1. Songs I Love 🎧

Music is always part of my day. I really enjoy listening to Jersey Club songs because of their catchy beats and fun rhythms. Some of my favorites are WYWMY SWEETY, ALL THAT MATTERS, and LET ME KNOW. These songs are definitely on my repeat playlist!


2. My Go-To Applications ✨

I love using creative apps, especially for editing, designing, and finding inspiration. My go-to apps are Canva, Snapseed, and CapCut. I also enjoy scrolling through Pinterest, Instagram, and TikTok to find new ideas and creative inspiration.


3. Movies I Enjoy 🍿

When it comes to movies, Romantic Comedy is definitely my favorite genre! I love stories that mix romance, humor, and a little drama. They make me smile, laugh, and sometimes get butterflies. A cute rom-com is always a good choice for my relaxing time!


A Little Bit of My World ♡

These little favorites may seem simple, but they are part of what makes me, me. They bring happiness, inspiration, and fun to my everyday life!
